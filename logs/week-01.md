# Week 1

**Dates:** 06-15 to 06-19

## Goals

- Meet with my advisor and confirm project scope.
- Turn the proposal into a short Week 1 to Week 3 plan.
- Define the minimum viable first milestone.

## Approach and Implementation

Week 1 was focused on advisor meetings and project planning based on the initial research proposal. In discussion with my advisor, we clarified the central question: whether a guarded, tool-using AI agent can improve spacecraft telemetry anomaly triage while reducing unsupported or unsafe conclusions.

I broke the project into four components from the plan: telemetry analytics, evidence extraction, agentic triage, and guardrails. We also agreed to keep scope controlled early by starting with one dataset, one baseline anomaly detector, and one structured triage output format.

I then mapped those decisions into short-term milestones so Week 2 could focus on dataset inquiry before implementation choices are finalized.

## Results

- Advisor alignment completed on project objective, constraints, and evaluation direction.
- A phased roadmap was created around baseline-first development and safety-focused evaluation.
- Week 2 priorities were defined around selecting and assessing candidate telemetry datasets.

The main output this week was a clear and realistic starting plan that prioritizes faithful, evidence-backed triage before expanding system complexity.

## Notes

- We explicitly decided to avoid overextending scope in the first milestone and prioritize reproducible setup and clear evaluation criteria.
